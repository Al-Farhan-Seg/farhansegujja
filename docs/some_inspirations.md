## Plan for extra two pages
My preferred combination is **Farhan Labs** and **Tooltown.**

**Farhan Labs** gives you permission to build anything interesting without worrying whether it has commercial potential. **Tooltown** becomes a collection of small, genuinely useful applications that people can return to every day.

You don't need separate domains initially. They can live at `/labs` and `/tools` on your existing website.
