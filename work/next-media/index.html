Context and role

Embiro Technologies was contracted to redesign the corporate website for Next Media. I handled the majority of the engineering implementation, working alongside another intern who contributed smaller changes and reviewed parts of the build.

What shipped

Brand ecosystem showcase

A homepage section presenting Next Media's full portfolio — 20+ properties across television, radio, digital news and sport.

Leadership presentation

A Board of Directors carousel and structured leadership pages (Exco, Top Management, Senior Management, Board).

Nile Post news integration

A live "Today on Nile Post" homepage section pulling recent headlines from Next Media's own news property via RSS.

Careers

Job openings and internship listings presented as structured, navigable content.

Navigation restructuring

A multi-brand navigation system covering broadcasting, radio, digital and corporate sections without overwhelming a first-time visitor.

Responsive layout

Desktop-to-mobile layout work across image-heavy sections — brand grids, carousels and leadership photography.
